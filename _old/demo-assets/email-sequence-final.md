## Email 2

**Subject:** The risk with a timing problem
**Preview text:** Why the same market drop can be harmless or permanent

Hi {{first_name}},

Quick thought experiment.

The market drops 30 percent. Is that a disaster?

Depends when. At 40, no. You have decades to recover and you're still buying, so lower prices actually help you. The year after you retire? Different story. Now you're selling shares at the bottom to pay real bills, and shares sold in a downturn never recover with the market.

This is sequence risk, and it's why we keep talking about the five-year window. The years right around your retirement date are where a bad stretch does lasting damage, and where a good plan earns its keep.

Nobody can tell you what markets will do. A plan's job is to make sure no single bad year gets to decide how you live.

Section two of the checklist walks through this for your own timeline.

Priya
