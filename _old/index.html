Will this replace my team?

It adds to them. The plan decides what your team should keep and finds a system or a person for the rest. Your team gets the credit for what ships.

What if we already have a strategy?

Then the Assessment puts it to the test. Some strategies need a new direction and some just need the right people behind them. Either way you'll know which, with the numbers to back it.

What if the answer is a hire?

Then we help you make a good one. We define the role, help find the person, and find contractors to keep the work moving while you hire.

Who owns what you build and who you find?

You do. Systems live in your accounts, contractors work with you directly, and hires go on your payroll. When we're done, we hand it all over.
