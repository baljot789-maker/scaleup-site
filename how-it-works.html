The ten days

Two hours of your time, start to finish

The audit is built so your team barely feels it. One kickoff, view-only access to what you already use, and a debrief. Everything between those is my work, not yours.

Day 1

Kickoff, 90 minutes the $4,500 starts here

You walk me through what your team actually does in a week and where it jams. I ask a lot of questions about the boring parts, because the boring parts are where the hours hide.

Then you hand over view-only access to the tools you already run. No admin rights, no changes to anything.

Days 2 to 7

I go count

I trace the real path of work through your stack: what gets done twice, what gets rebuilt every month, what a tool you're paying for already covers. Ninety days of shipped work against what your team says each piece takes, with the waiting time read off your timestamps.

You get one note from me around day 5 with what I'm seeing so far, so a wrong read gets corrected early rather than at the debrief. Other than that your team hears nothing.

Days 8 and 9

The blueprints get written

Each system on the kill list gets a blueprint detailed enough to hand to any developer: what it does, what it connects to, what it costs to run, and how you'd know it's working.

Day 10

Debrief, 30 minutes

I hand over all five documents plus the recorded walkthrough, and answer whatever the documents raised. If you want a build, we scope it here. If you don't, you still keep everything.
