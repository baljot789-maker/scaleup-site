About

Wherever I go, my job is connecting the dots

Ten years in marketing. I started as a copywriter and strategist in Mississippi, moved agency-side running client programs, and spent the last several years in-house running departments. So I have been the freelancer, the agency, and the client.

That's the whole reason this business exists. I have been the person in the marketing department holding a plan I believed in and watching it sit there, and I learned to find help that needed one person's approval: mine. I have been doing it for years.

I write Python and HTML, so I build the thing rather than describing it to someone who will. That matters more than it sounds. It means the estimate and the build come from the same person, and it means I can tell you early when something is better left alone.

The best work still comes from human thinking. Original ideas, real perspective, genuine connection. Machines should take the repetitive and the mechanical so your people spend their week on the work only they can do. Every system I build is designed around that line.
