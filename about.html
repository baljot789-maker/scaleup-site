About

Wherever I go, my job is connecting the dots

I'm Joe. I've spent over a decade in marketing, and I've scaled every program I've run, whether that came through outsourcing, automation, or just deleting work that never needed doing.

Most teams are sitting on tools, talent and data they aren't fully using. I find where things break down and build the fix. Sometimes that's a system. Sometimes it's a spreadsheet and a hard conversation about a subscription nobody opens.

I write Python and HTML, so I build the thing rather than describing it to someone who will. That matters more than it sounds. It means the estimate and the build come from the same person, and it means I can tell you when something isn't worth building.

I believe the best work still comes from human thinking. Original ideas, real perspective, genuine connection. Machines should take the repetitive and the mechanical so your people can do the work only they can do. Every system I build is designed around that line.

Why I built this

Every marketing team I have joined had the same problem underneath the surface problem. Nobody could say where the week actually went, so every decision about tools, headcount and priorities was a guess dressed up as a plan.

The audit exists because that is the cheapest thing to fix and almost nobody does it. Once you can see the week, what to build becomes obvious, and half of what you thought you needed turns out not to be worth building. I would rather tell you that in fifteen minutes than sell you something.
